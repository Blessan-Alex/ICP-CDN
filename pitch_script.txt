## SLIDE 2: Problem & Market (90 seconds)
**Speaker: [Technical Lead]**

"Let me start by addressing the elephant in the room: why do we need another CDN?

The current CDN market is dominated by centralized players like Cloudflare and AWS CloudFront, charging $0.085 per GB with complete control over your content. This is a $25 billion market growing 15% annually, but it has fundamental problems.

First, it's expensive. Traditional CDNs charge per GB transferred, which adds up quickly for high-traffic applications. Second, there's vendor lock-in - once you're on a platform, switching is painful and costly. Third, geographic limitations - content delivery depends on the CDN's infrastructure, not your users' needs.

But most importantly, these are single points of failure. When Cloudflare goes down, thousands of websites go down with it. We've seen this happen multiple times in the past year alone.

This is exactly the kind of problem ICP was designed to solve - decentralized, resilient, and cost-effective infrastructure."

## SLIDE 3: Solution & Demo Snapshot (120 seconds)
**Speaker: [Frontend Developer]**

"CanisterDrop solves these problems by being the first truly native ICP Content Delivery Network. Here's what makes us different:

Our core innovation is that everything happens on-chain. We're not just wrapping existing CDN services - we're building a CDN from the ground up using ICP's native capabilities. This means HTTP outcalls to IPFS gateways, intelligent LRU caching within canisters, and cycles-based billing that's transparent and fair.

Let me show you our demo. [DEMO STARTS] Here you can see our drag-and-drop upload interface. Users can upload files directly to our system, and we automatically handle the rest. Notice the real-time progress tracking and the intelligent caching system that optimizes delivery.

Our system processes images on-chain, resizing them in real-time based on user requirements. The content is then delivered through our intelligent caching layer, with automatic fallback to IPFS if needed. All of this happens in seconds, with costs measured in cycles rather than dollars."

## SLIDE 4: How It Works (Architecture) (120 seconds)
**Speaker: [Backend Developer]**

"Let me walk you through our architecture, which is what makes CanisterDrop truly special.

At the core, we have a Rust canister that handles all the heavy lifting. This canister makes HTTP outcalls directly to IPFS gateways and the Pinata API, eliminating the need for external servers. Everything is on-chain, which means it's decentralized, secure, and cost-effective.

Our frontend is a modern React application with Internet Identity authentication, providing a seamless user experience. But here's the game-changer: we've built a complete client library that allows other ICP projects to integrate our dCDN as a service.

This means OpenChat can add content delivery capabilities with just a few lines of code. Caffeine can integrate our image processing. Any ICP dApp can use our infrastructure without building their own CDN. This is the power of canister-to-canister communication."
