## 🎥 **RECORDING TIPS**

### **Camera Setup**
- **Position**: Eye level, slight angle
- **Distance**: 3-4 feet from camera
- **Background**: Clean, uncluttered
- **Clothing**: Professional, solid colors
- **Movement**: Minimal, natural gestures

### **Audio Quality**
- **Microphone**: Close to speaker (6-12 inches)
- **Levels**: -12dB to -6dB peak
- **Environment**: Eliminate background noise
- **Backup**: Record audio separately
- **Test**: Record 30 seconds and review

### **Lighting**
- **Main Light**: Front, slightly above
- **Fill Light**: Side, reduce shadows
- **Back Light**: Behind, separate from background
- **Natural Light**: Face window, avoid backlighting
- **Consistency**: Same lighting for all speakers

### **Screen Recording (Demo)**
- **Software**: OBS Studio, Camtasia, or similar
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps or higher
- **Audio**: Include system audio
- **Backup**: Record demo separately

## 📝 **SCRIPT DELIVERY TIPS**

### **Speaking Style**
- **Pace**: 150-160 words per minute
- **Tone**: Enthusiastic but professional

- **Emphasis**: Key points and numbers
- **Eye Contact**: Look at camera, not screen

### **Body Language**
- **Posture**: Upright, confident
- **Gestures**: Natural, supporting speech
- **Movement**: Minimal, purposeful
- **Expression**: Engaged, passionate
- **Energy**: High but controlled

### **Technical Terms**
- **Pronunciation**: Practice ICP terms
- **Definitions**: Explain complex concepts
- **Examples**: Use concrete examples
- **Numbers**: Emphasize key statistics
- **Names**: Correct pronunciation of tools
